  training/download_data.py      - Auto-downloads RAVDESS + CREMA-D
  training/create_style_refs.py  - Copies RAVDESS clips → style_refs/

  pipeline/translator.py         - Loads trained NMT, translates English → Hindi
  pipeline/translator_pretrained.py - Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-hi fallback (BLEU ~31)
